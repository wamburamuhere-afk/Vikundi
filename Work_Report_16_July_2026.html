Vikundi · VICOBA Group Management System

Work Report — Member Experience & Security

Three things this day: a new personal home screen that gives members a reason to open the system, a thorough security review that protects the group's money, and a clearer leadership dashboard.
Prepared for: Management Date: 16 July 2026 Status: Live & in use

1 · At a glance

Today's work covered three areas. First, we gave members a personal home screen — a clear, encouraging view of their own savings that gives them a reason to log in regularly. Second, during our own testing we discovered and fixed a serious security gap, then reviewed and protected every money function in the system. Third, we made the leadership dashboard clearer and more consistent.

2 · New for members — a personal home screen New

Until now, a member logging in did not have a place that simply showed them their own money. They do now. When a member signs in, they land on a personal home page showing, at a glance:

Total savings

Everything they have saved with the group, and how much this year.

Fines due

Any outstanding fines, clearly shown (and zero when they're clear).

Next meeting

The date, time and place of the group's next meeting.

A 12-month savings chart

A simple bar chart of their saving over the past year.

🔥 A savings streak

A gentle, encouraging counter of how many months in a row they have saved — for example, "9 months in a row." It is private to each member: there is no public ranking and no one is shamed. It simply rewards consistency, which is exactly what a savings group depends on.

Why it matters: members who can see their savings grow — and their streak build — come back more often and stay engaged. It also reinforces the group's transparency: every member can see their own numbers, and those numbers match the official records exactly.

3 · Making it work properly Fixes

As we tested the new home screen on the live system, we found and corrected a few things so the experience is smooth and trustworthy:

  • Members now land on their home screen when they log in (leadership continue to the management dashboard, as before).
  • The "My statement" link works and opens the member's own statement. We also closed a related privacy gap so that a member can only ever see their own statement, never another member's.
  • The savings chart now displays its bars correctly.
  • Every figure on the home screen is drawn from a single source, so a member's savings total is identical on their home screen, their statement, and any letter produced for them.

4 · Security — protecting the group's money Important

What we found. During testing we discovered that an ordinary member — not a leader — was able to add and even delete the group's expense records. The screens correctly hid these actions from members, but the underlying system was not checking permission when a request came in, so the protection could be bypassed. This is a serious risk to the group's financial integrity.

What we did. We fixed every affected expense function so that only leadership can create or delete expenses and petty-cash vouchers, and we strengthened the system against forged requests. We then confirmed on the live system: a member attempting to add or delete an expense is now firmly refused ("you do not have permission"), while leadership keep full access.

To be clear: we found this ourselves through careful testing and closed it. We then went further and reviewed every money-related function in the system.

The full review — results. The same kind of gap existed on a few more functions (editing and approving expenses, editing an accounting entry, and bulk-uploading contributions); all are now protected the same way. We also stopped a member from being able to file a contribution against another member — a member may now only submit their own. The good news: fines and funeral-aid payouts were already properly protected, and every fix was confirmed on the live system.

5 · A clearer leadership dashboard Improved

We refreshed the leadership dashboard so it reads clearly and matches the new member screen:

  • A cleaner, consistent look — the summary tiles (members, balance, expenses, contributions, budget) now use the same clean style as the member home, so the whole system feels like one product.
  • Money in vs money out — the monthly chart now shows contributions and expenses side by side, so leadership can see at a glance whether the group ran a surplus or a deficit each month, instead of only how much came in.

6 · What's next

  • Post-meeting notifications (an SMS to each member after a meeting, with their contribution and total) — ready to build once the cost and member opt-in are agreed.
  • A small decision on the old loans feature, which is switched off — either fully lock its unused functions or remove them.
In short: members now have a personal, encouraging view of their savings; the group's money functions have been reviewed and properly protected across the board; and the leadership dashboard is clearer, showing money in against money out at a glance.
