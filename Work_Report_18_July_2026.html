Work Report — Interface Polish & Consistency

Today's work was a careful clean-up pass across the system: fixing a menu that was hard to use, and making the way money is shown the same everywhere. Small things on their own — but together they make the system feel noticeably more finished and trustworthy to members and leaders.
Prepared for: Management Date: 18 July 2026 Status: Live & in use

1 · At a glance

The system already does a lot. Today we focused on polish — the details that decide whether software feels professional or rough. Four improvements were made: the row action menus now open fully instead of being cut off, and the currency is shown the same way everywhere — across the dashboard, all the reports, the members' contribution and fines pages, and the funeral-support screen. Every change was checked on the live site.

2 · Action menus that were being cut off Fixed

On every list in the system — members, budgets, fines, expenses and the rest — each row has a small gear button that opens a menu of actions (View, Print, Edit, Delete, and so on).

Before

When a list was short, opening that menu got trapped inside the table and a scrollbar appeared — so the lower options were hidden until you scrolled. It looked broken and made simple actions fiddly.

After

The menu now opens in full, every time, on every list in the system. It was one fix applied in a shared place, so the whole system benefited at once — and wide tables still scroll sideways as before.

3 · One currency label across the whole system Consistency

The problem. The same currency was being shown two different ways. Some screens said “TSh” (the dashboard totals), while charts and every report said “TZS”. To anyone reading carefully, it looked like two different things — exactly the kind of small inconsistency that makes people question the numbers.

Fixed. Everything now reads “TSh” — the dashboard chart, the group report, the expense report, the member statement and the funeral-support analysis. One currency, shown one way, everywhere leadership looks.

4 · The members' money pages, made consistent Consistency

The two pages members and leaders use most for money — the Contribution Ledger and the Fines page — were the worst offenders, and were tidied up:

  • They now show “TSh” like the rest of the system, and always in the same position (before the amount). Previously the label sat after the number in some places and before it in others — even on the same page.
  • On the Fines list, the amounts were showing as bare numbers with no currency at all (e.g. “10,000.00”). They now clearly read “TSh 10,000.00”, so there is no doubt what the figure means.

5 · A corrected summary on funeral support Fixed

The Funeral / Death Assistance page has three summary boxes at the top. One of them was mislabelled.

Before

A box titled “Cases This Month” — which should be a simple count — was actually showing a money amount, and displayed oddly as “0.00”. The title and the number did not agree.

After

The box is now correctly titled “Paid This Month” and shows a proper money figure — for example “TSh 0.00” — matching the total-assistance box beside it. Title and number now agree.

6 · How it was checked, and what's next

  • Every change was checked on the live site against the group's real data, and each is covered by automated tests so the behaviour is protected going forward.
  • The interface now speaks with one voice on money — the same currency label on every screen a member or leader can reach.
  • Still on the list from earlier: post-meeting SMS notifications (awaiting cost and member opt-in sign-off) and a small decision on the switched-off loans feature.
In short: the system is cleaner and more consistent today — action menus open properly on every list, money is shown the same way everywhere, and a mislabelled summary on the funeral-support page now reads correctly. Nothing flashy; just a system that looks and behaves like it was built with care.
