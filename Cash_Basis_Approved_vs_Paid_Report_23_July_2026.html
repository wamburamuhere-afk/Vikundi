Approved vs Paid — Cash-Basis Money Tracking

The system now separates money that is authorised from money that has actually left the account. The group Balance reflects real cash only; anything approved-but-not-yet-paid is shown clearly beside it, so nothing is hidden and nothing is double-counted.
Prepared for: Management Group: UKUU MSAKUZI Date: 23 July 2026 Status: Live & in use

You raised a simple but important point: "some things may be approved but not yet paid." A funeral support or an expense can be approved (the committee has said yes) while the money is still in the account — it hasn't been handed over yet. The system now treats those two moments as different things, the way a real treasurer does.

The one rule that matters

The Balance only changes when money is actually PAID. Approving an expense does not reduce the Balance — it simply records that the group has promised to pay it. The Balance drops the moment the treasurer marks it Paid. This is called cash-basis accounting: the Balance is always the real cash the group is holding.

Only the last step touches the Balance. Everything before it is just paperwork moving forward.

Why you saw a number change on approval

When you approved a test expense, a figure on the dashboard did move — but it was the "Expenses" card, not the "Balance" card. These two answer different questions:

CardWhat it answersDoes approving change it?
Balance How much cash does the group actually hold right now? No — only paying changes it
Expenses How much has the group authorised to spend in total? Yes — it counts approved and paid

So the system was already behaving correctly. The Balance held steady on approval; the Expenses total went up because the group had now committed to that spending.

What the Balance card now shows a leader

A treasurer opening the dashboard now sees the full money position at a glance:

  • Balance — real cash held (money in, minus money actually paid out).
  • Approved, not yet paid — money the group has promised out but hasn't handed over (a payable). Appears only when there is something outstanding.
  • Fines owed, not yet collected — money members owe the group that hasn't come in yet (a receivable). The mirror image of the above.

Nothing is hidden

Because approved-but-unpaid money is shown next to the Balance instead of quietly removed from it, the committee can see both the true cash and the promises against it — no surprises at pay-out time.

Who can mark things Paid

Recording the actual disbursement is a money control, so it is limited to the Treasurer and Admin only. Other roles can record and review expenses, but confirming that cash has left the account is the treasurer's responsibility — exactly as it is in the group's book.

A stability fix found along the way

Report pages no longer error on empty data

While verifying the work on the live site, an old pop-up error ("Incorrect column count") appeared on some report pages when a table had no records yet — common on a fresh group. This was traced to three report tables and fixed; they now show a clean "No records yet" message instead of an error.
