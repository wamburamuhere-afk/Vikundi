Membership Onboarding

Bringing UKUU MSAKUZI's real members — and their savings history — out of the M-Koba mobile records and into the system, so the group's own books and reports now reflect the actual membership.
Prepared for: Management Group: UKUU MSAKUZI Date: 21 July 2026 Status: Loaded & verified

1 · At a glance

Until now the system had been running on sample data — example members and figures used while it was being built and tested. Today we replaced that with the real thing: the group's actual members and their real savings history, taken from the M-Koba records and loaded into the system.

2 · What was done

  1. A full backup first. Before touching anything, we took a complete backup of the system, kept safely aside — so every step could be undone if needed.
  2. Cleared the sample data. The 30 example members and their practice figures were removed, so the system would hold only the group's real records. All the setup — roles, settings, accounts and so on — was left fully intact.
  3. Loaded the real members. All 327 members from the M-Koba records were brought in — each with their name and phone number, and set up with their own account in the system.
  4. Loaded their savings history. Every one of the 524 contributions — totalling TSh 7,968,000 — was imported and automatically matched to the right member by phone number, keeping the original date and receipt of each payment.
  5. Queued for the committee. Each new member now appears under "New Requests" for leadership to formally approve — a simple final sign-off that activates them.

3 · Done carefully

Every figure was checked and matched — 327 members in, 327 accounted for; 524 payments in, every one attached to a member with none left unmatched. Nothing outside the intended data was touched, the backup is kept safe, and the sensitive member file was removed from the server once the load was complete.

4 · What's next

  • Leadership approves the members — the 327 New Requests are reviewed and approved (can be done in one action).
  • The treasurer confirms the savings — approving the imported contributions so they count towards the group's balance.
  • Once both are done, the dashboard, member list and reports all reflect the real group: 327 members and TSh 7,968,000 in recorded savings.
In short: the group's real membership and savings history are now in the system — accurately, in full, and safely — and only need leadership's approval to go fully live. The move from sample data to the real group is done.
