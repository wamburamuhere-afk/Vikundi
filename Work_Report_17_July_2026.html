Work Report — A Trustworthy Activity Record

Today's work rebuilt the system's activity record — the log of who did what, and when — into a proper, professional audit trail the group can rely on: it now catches break-in attempts, stays clear instead of cluttered, and lets leadership review any member's activity at a glance.
Prepared for: Management Date: 17 July 2026 Status: Live & in use

1 · At a glance

Every action in the system — a login, a contribution, an edit, a deletion — is quietly recorded in an activity log. That log is what lets the group answer "who did this, and when". Today we took that log from a basic list and made it professional, across six areas: it now records failed login attempts, shows the events that matter instead of browsing noise, allows a complete download for records, loads quickly as it grows, gives leadership real review tools, and can show one member's whole day as a clear timeline.

2 · Catching attempts to break in Security

The gap. Until now the system only recorded successful logins. If someone repeatedly guessed a member's or leader's password, they left no trace at all — the single most important security signal was invisible.

Fixed. Every failed sign-in attempt is now recorded — a wrong password, an unknown account, or someone trying to reach a suspended account. The password itself is never stored; only the attempt and the reason. Failed attempts stand out in red, so a burst of them is easy to spot.

3 · A clear record, not a noisy one Improved

The system logs every page a person opens, which meant the record was roughly nine-tenths "someone looked at a screen" — real events like money movements and deletions were buried underneath.

  • The activity view now shows the meaningful events by default — logins, contributions, edits, deletions — and hides routine page-browsing behind a simple "include page views" switch for when it's needed. Nothing is lost; it's just no longer in the way.
  • Every entry now shows the time down to the exact second, which matters when two actions happen close together.

4 · Complete records you can keep Fixed

The "Export" button used to save only the 25 rows visible on the screen — so an "exported record" was really just one page, not much use for a review or a handover. It now downloads the complete set of records that match whatever you're looking at (by date, by person, by type), as a proper spreadsheet file — including the network address each action came from. The system also notes in the log that a download was taken.

5 · Fast even as it grows Improved

The activity log grows every single day. We tuned the underlying database so searching and filtering it stays fast as it grows into hundreds of thousands of entries, rather than slowing the page down over time. This was applied to the live system automatically during today's release — nothing manual required.

6 · Tools to actually review it New

A record is only useful if leadership can question it. Two additions turn the log from a list into a review tool:

Filter by action

Show me every deletion · every login · every failed login — in one click, alongside the existing filters for person, type and date.

An at-a-glance summary

A row of totals at the top — logins, failed logins, items created, edited and deleted — for whatever you're viewing. Failed logins turn a bold red the moment there is even one.

7 · One member's day, as a story New

The biggest addition. Leadership can now pick any member and see their activity as a readable timeline instead of scattered lines: each visit is grouped into a session — when they signed in, everything they did (with the exact time), and when they signed out — along with how long they were active and how many actions they took. It answers, cleanly, "what did this person do, and when?"

Example. A single session reads: signed in at 1:45 pm → downloaded a report → downloaded it again → a failed login was recorded at 2:17 pm — 32 minutes, 4 actions — laid out in order, so the whole visit is understood at a glance.

8 · Status & next

  • All six improvements are built, tested and released to the live system today, and were checked on the live site.
  • Each change is covered by automated tests, so the behaviour is protected against future changes.
  • Still on the list from earlier: post-meeting SMS notifications (awaiting cost/opt-in sign-off) and a small decision on the switched-off loans feature.
In short: the group's activity record is now a trustworthy audit trail — it catches attempts to break in, shows what truly matters instead of clutter, can be downloaded in full, stays fast as it grows, and lets leadership review any member's activity, session by session.
